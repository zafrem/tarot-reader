# Image Credits

The 78 card images in `images/` are the Rider-Waite-Smith tarot deck
(first published 1909 by William Rider & Son), illustrated by
**Pamela Colman Smith**. The deck is in the public domain in the United
States and the United Kingdom.

Source: the "Roses & Lilies" scan set, via Wikimedia Commons —
[Category:Rider-Waite tarot deck (Roses & Lilies)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite_tarot_deck_(Roses_%26_Lilies)).
Each file's individual page on Commons documents the same
public-domain status (`Public domain` per Wikimedia's licensing
metadata).

Images were downloaded at 600px width (Wikimedia's thumbnail rendition)
to keep package size reasonable; originals are higher resolution and
available at the Commons links above if needed.
